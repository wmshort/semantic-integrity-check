# Course Extension Policy

## 1. Eligibility

An "Eligible Student" means a student currently enrolled on a credit-bearing
module. An Eligible Student may request an extension to a coursework deadline.
Extensions are granted at the Programme Director's discretion and are not
automatic.

## 2. Deadlines and limits

A request must be submitted at least 3 working days before the original deadline,
and an extension may not exceed 14 calendar days. A late-submission penalty of 5%
per day applies to work submitted without an approved extension.

## 3. Conditions

An extension may be granted only if the student provides supporting evidence,
unless the Programme Director waives this requirement in exceptional circumstances.
Requests submitted after the original deadline will not be considered.

## 4. Communication

Staff should describe the outcome factually and should not guarantee approval
before the Programme Director has decided.
